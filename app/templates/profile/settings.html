Data Verification Pipeline Workflow

AllerGraph enforces a three-stage verification lifecycle for food data integrity:

  1. Ingestion: New ingredients enter as UNVERIFIED by default.
  2. Editorial Review: Editors link lab analysis or supplier spec sheets and update status to IN_REVIEW.
  3. Verification Approval: Timestamped VERIFIED flag awarded upon certificate audit.
